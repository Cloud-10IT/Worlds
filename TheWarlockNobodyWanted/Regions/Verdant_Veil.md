## Overview

The Verdant Veil is an enchanted realm where the boundary separating the material world and the Fey Realm has dissolved almost entirely.

An ancient, glowing forest enveloped in an eternal magical spring, the realm is aggressively defended by elite Rangers and woodland guardians.

Time and distance behave unpredictably beneath the shimmering canopy, disorienting unwanted intruders while welcoming those who respect the natural order.

## Scene Rules

Whenever the Verdant Veil appears:

Always Include:

- Dense, vibrant forest environments
- Soft bioluminescence or magical lighting
- Signs of living wood structures
- Overgrown, untouched natural beauty

Frequently Include:

- Rangers in camouflaged gear
- Hidden rope bridges and high canopy posts
- Crystal-clear streams
- Exotic woodland wildlife

---

## Consistency Rules

Always Preserve:

- Eternal spring feel
- Fey and Ranger identity
- Living wood architecture
- Bioluminescent highlights

Never Generate:

- Clear-cut barren lands
- Heavy stone fortresses
- Industrial smoke or machinery
- Volcanic, desert, or frozen terrain
