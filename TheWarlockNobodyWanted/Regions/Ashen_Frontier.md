## Overview

The Ashen Frontier is a vast and dangerous land scarred by centuries of warfare.

Ancient kingdoms once fought across these plains before collapsing into ruin.

The landscape remains marked by forgotten battlefields, shattered fortresses, and mass graves.

Many believe the frontier itself remembers the dead.

The region is feared by travelers and avoided by most civilized nations.
