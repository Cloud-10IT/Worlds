## Overview

The Shadow Coast is a dangerous network of coastal cities, marshland settlements, hidden coves, and thriving ports.

Official trade fuels the region's economy, but much of its wealth comes from smuggling, espionage, and clandestine dealings.

Information is often more valuable than gold.

Every city contains layers of visible authority and hidden influence.

Power is rarely held openly.

- Merchant districts

## Relationship To The World

The Shadow Coast serves as a major gateway for maritime trade.

Many nations rely on its ports.

Many rulers distrust its people.

Both opinions are justified.
