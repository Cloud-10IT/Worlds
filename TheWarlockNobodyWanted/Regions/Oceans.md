# AZURE OCEAN

## World Region Information

Name:
Azure Ocean

Classification:
Global Ocean System

Status:
Permanent Geographic Feature

Coverage:
Surrounds all major continents

Importance:
Primary trade network connecting the world

---

## Overview

The Azure Ocean is the vast body of water connecting every major continent and civilization.

Countless islands, reefs, hidden coves, and unexplored waters exist throughout its enormous expanse.

The ocean serves as the backbone of international trade, exploration, naval warfare, and cultural exchange.

Although heavily traveled near established trade routes, much of the Azure Ocean remains unexplored.

Many sailors believe more secrets exist beneath its waves than upon the continents themselves.

## Consistency Rules

Always Preserve:

- Massive scale
- Trade importance
- Island networks
- Sea monster presence
- Ancient underwater mysteries

Never Generate:

- Tiny enclosed seas
- Modern vessels
- Futuristic technology
- Industrial shipping lanes
- Modern ports

The Azure Ocean must always feel ancient, mysterious, dangerous, and vital to the world's civilizations.
