## Overview

Aeloria is the central crown jewel of civilization and high magic in the known world. 

Constructed upon glowing ley-line nodes, the realm stands as a monument to refined arcane power, grand scholarship, and spiritual mastery. 

Unlike the volcanic arcane focus of the Ember Crown, Aeloria represents organized, harmonious, and celestial magic. 

It serves as the international diplomatic hub where scholars, mages, and high priests gather to record history, regulate spellcraft, and maintain balance across all continental borders.
