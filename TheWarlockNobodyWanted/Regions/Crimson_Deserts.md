## Overview

The Crimson Deserts are a vast and unforgiving wilderness known for colossal monsters, ancient buried civilizations, and elite hunters.

The deserts stretch beyond the horizon in every direction, broken only by enormous canyons, hidden oases, and the ruins of forgotten empires buried beneath centuries of shifting sands.

Survival is a way of life.

Only the strongest endure.

---

## Consistency Rules

Always Preserve:

- Crimson sand
- Massive dunes
- Ancient ruins
- Monster hunter culture
- Hot climate
- Sandstone architecture
- Large canyon systems

Never Generate:

- Tropical jungles
- Snow fields
- Green forests
- Modern cities
- Futuristic technology
- Skyscrapers
- Industrial environments

The Crimson Deserts must always feel ancient, dangerous, beautiful, and shaped by both monsters and the relentless desert environment.
