## Overview

The Ember Crown is a powerful arcane kingdom built amid active volcanic ranges and rivers of molten lava.

Magic is woven into nearly every aspect of life. Social standing is determined primarily through magical knowledge, arcane achievements, and mastery of spellcraft.

The kingdom is considered one of the greatest centers of magical research in the known world.

Ancient magical academies and towering arcane citadels dominate the landscape.
