## Overview

The Golden Sea is a prosperous kingdom known for its fertile lands, powerful holy orders, and unwavering faith.

The region serves as the spiritual center of the known world, producing many of the greatest Paladins, priests, and holy warriors.

Its vast golden plains feed neighboring kingdoms while its knightly armies defend against darkness wherever it emerges.

The people view honor, faith, and duty as the foundations of civilization.
