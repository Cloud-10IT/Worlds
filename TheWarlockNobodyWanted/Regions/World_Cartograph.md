## 2. Shadow Coast Settlement Directory

**Bradenwood** (Coastal Port City) serves as the western anchor point for the Shadow Coast. Surrounding settlements connect via primary trade roads, secondary trails, and deepwater sea routes:

### Low-Level Outposts & Villages
* **Oakhaven**: Logging outpost situated on the dense border of the Wildheart Expanse.
* **Whisperglen**: Coastal fishing hamlet hidden along foggy marsh channels and tidal flats.
* **Windscar**: Wind-swept watchtower post guarding the mountain pass into the Iron Peaks.
* **Mossrock**: River-crossing outpost handling local barge traffic and inland trade.

### Mid-Tier Towns & Trade Hubs
* **Aethelport**: Secondary fortified harbor routing legal trade and merchant fleets into Blackharbor.
* **Silvermere**: Mining town built near crystalline lake beds at the base of the mountain range.
* **Ghostwood**: Fortified woodland settlement constructed near ancient, fog-shrouded ruin sites.

### Grand Strongholds & Castles
* **Ironfist Fortress**: Heavily fortified mountain keep holding the eastern border against mountain threats.
* **Serpent's Point**: Coastal cliffside castle guarding the southern maritime approach and naval trade.
* **Blackharbor**: Capital fortress-city featuring massive dockyard networks, merchant guilds, and hidden underground tunnels.

---

## 3. Primary Continental Trade Routes

* **The Shadow Highway**: Connects Bradenwood through Aethelport, curving past Mossrock to Ironfist Fortress.
* **The Pilgrim Way**: Runs from Solhaven across the Golden Sea directly to the central marble gates of Aeloria Prime.
* **The Ash Trade Run**: Connects Sandspire across the Crimson Deserts to the northern borders of the Ashen Frontier.
* **The Northern Barrier Line**: Patrol roads running through Stoneguard in the Iron Peaks, dividing civilization from the Forbidden North.
* **Azure Ocean Sea Lanes**: Deepwater shipping lanes connecting Blackharbor (Shadow Coast), Aurum Bay (Crimson Deserts), and Aeloria's coastal docks.
