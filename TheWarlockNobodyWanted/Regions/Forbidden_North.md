## Overview

The Forbidden North is a vast frozen wilderness beyond the Iron Peaks.

No kingdom officially claims the region.

Most maps terminate at its borders.

The territory is considered one of the most dangerous and mysterious locations in the world.

Ancient ruins buried beneath ice and snow suggest that powerful civilizations once existed here before disappearing without explanation.

Many expeditions have entered the region.

Few have returned.

## Legends

Ancient stories claim the first great civilizations existed here.

Some historians believe:

- The first Warlocks originated here
- Ancient empires ruled these lands
- Lost magic remains buried beneath the glaciers

Most modern scholars reject these theories.

Official records deny any connection.

Persistent evidence continues to fuel debate.
