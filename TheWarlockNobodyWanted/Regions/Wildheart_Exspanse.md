## Overview

The Wildheart Expanse is one of the oldest regions in the world.

Ancient forests stretch for thousands of miles, untouched by large-scale civilization.

Gigantic world trees tower above the canopy, some believed to have existed since before recorded history.

The inhabitants live in harmony with nature and maintain a deep connection to spirits, beasts, and the natural world.

Many believe the forests themselves are alive and aware.

Many never find what they seek.

The forests reveal their secrets only to those they deem worthy.
