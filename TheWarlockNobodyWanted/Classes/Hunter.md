# Hunter

> **Canon status:** World Bible class file  
> **Series:** *The Warlock Nobody Wanted*  
> **Rule:** This file defines the permanent baseline for the Hunter class. New abilities may be added only through documented progression, relics, training, bloodlines, or major canon events. New material must not contradict `00_MASTER_CANON.md`.

## Core Role

Ranged damage dealer, tracker, survivalist, and bonded companion specialist.

## Social Prestige

**Middle Prestige**

## Place in Society

Hunters patrol borders, map dangerous lands, eliminate monsters, and escort travelers through hostile regions.

Unawakened people may hold political, military, religious, or economic authority over members of this class. Having a class does not automatically grant noble rank, legal immunity, wealth, leadership, or moral superiority.

## Recognized Specializations

- Marksman: precision shooting, long-range elimination, and battlefield overwatch.
- Beast Warden: bonds with natural or elemental companions.
- Pathfinder: traps, tracking, concealment, and wilderness survival.

## Standard Capabilities

- Expert use of bows, crossbows, thrown weapons, and selected firearms if canon later permits
- Tracking by physical, magical, and environmental signs
- Bonding with one or more beasts or elemental companions
- Traps, snares, ambushes, and terrain exploitation
- Long-range support and target marking

## Limitations and Costs

- Companion bonds take time and cannot be created instantly.
- A slain or injured bonded companion has lasting consequences.
- Hunters are vulnerable when pinned in close quarters.
- Ammunition, visibility, wind, and terrain affect performance.

## Rank Progression

- **F Rank:** Newly awakened; basic control and limited practical use.
- **E Rank:** Reliable foundational techniques.
- **D Rank:** Professionally capable; common ceiling for many Awakened.
- **C Rank:** Veteran-level skill and regional recognition.
- **B Rank:** Elite specialist capable of influencing major battles.
- **A Rank:** National asset whose actions affect kingdoms.
- **S Rank:** Exceptionally rare power capable of changing a nation's fate.
- **Legend Rank:** Historically significant individual.
- **Mythic Rank:** Power associated with world-defining events.
- **Transcendent Rank:** Believed by most to be mythical; any confirmed case requires explicit canon approval.

Rank increases capability, control, endurance, and scale. Rank does not remove established weaknesses, costs, training requirements, or consequences.

## Equipment and Training

Equipment must match the character's region, resources, profession, rank, and history. Rare artifacts require provenance. Mastery requires training, experience, or a documented extraordinary cause.

## Cinematic Visual Language

Layered travel gear, ranged weapons, animal or elemental companions, sweeping wilderness shots, and precise kinetic action.

Maintain widescreen **16:9 movie composition**. Keep costume, weapon, spell-effect, injury, and ability continuity across scenes. Do not redesign a recurring character between scenes unless the story documents the change.

## Canon Guardrails

1. This class cannot use another class's signature powers without an explicitly documented multiclass rule, relic, bloodline, pact, or major event.
2. Class abilities must obey the current rank, training, physical condition, magical reserves, and environment.
3. No unexplained power increases, new transformations, new companions, or new signature techniques.
4. Injuries, exhaustion, broken equipment, lost companions, vows, corruption, and resource costs persist until resolved in canon.
5. A class does not determine morality. Members may be heroic, villainous, neutral, loyal, corrupt, compassionate, or self-serving.
6. The existence of Unawakened rulers, soldiers, villagers, merchants, scholars, and nobles must remain respected.
7. Any permanent alteration to geography or climate requires an approved event under `00_MASTER_CANON.md` and must be recorded in `CONTINUITY_STATE.md` and `Timeline.md`.
8. When class lore conflicts with higher-authority canon, the higher-authority source wins.
