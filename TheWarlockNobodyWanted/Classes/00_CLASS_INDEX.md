# The Twelve Great Classes

> Drop this file into the `Classes` folder with the individual class files.

## Class Index

- [Warrior](Warrior.md): Versatile frontline combatant specializing in weapons, armor, protection, and sustained close-range damage.
- [Hunter](Hunter.md): Ranged damage dealer, tracker, survivalist, and bonded companion specialist.
- [Mage](Mage.md): Arcane spellcaster specializing in elemental and structured magical disciplines.
- [Monk](Monk.md): Martial artist who refines body, breath, and spirit to defend, heal, or inflict damage.
- [Paladin](Paladin.md): Holy champion capable of protection, healing, righteous offense, or defensive command.
- [Priest](Priest.md): Holy, disciplined, or shadow-aligned spellcaster focused on healing, protection, spiritual influence, and controlled damage.
- [Rogue](Rogue.md): Infiltrator, scout, assassin, saboteur, and burst-damage specialist.
- [Shaman](Shaman.md): Elemental and nature-linked magic user who communicates with spirits and can heal or deal damage.
- [Warlock](Warlock.md): A publicly disgraced class believed to use dark magic and demonic allies; its lost original form is the Sovereign commander class.
- [Death Knight](Death_Knight.md): Elite armored warrior using death magic, battlefield pressure, and defensive endurance.
- [Demon Hunter](Demon_Hunter.md): Rare specialist trained to hunt demons and corrupted entities using dangerous forbidden power.
- [Druid](Druid.md): Nature shapeshifter capable of healing, defense, scouting, and damage through multiple forms.

## Canon Note

The class files are subordinate to `00_MASTER_CANON.md`, `CONTINUITY_STATE.md`, and `Timeline.md`. They define baseline class rules and must not be used to overwrite established story events.
