## NARRATIVE & CINEMATIC SEQUENCE

### 1. CINEMATOGRAPHY & CAMERA MOTION
* **Camera Language:** Extreme High-Angle Aerial Zoom transitioning into a dynamic 360-degree Orbit to Low-Angle Tracking Shot.
* **Camera Path Execution:**
  * **Phase A (0s–3s):** High aerial wide shot looking down from 150 feet above the northern forest road (`K` border). Rapid, smooth downward zoom toward Aldric walking south.
  * **Phase B (3s–6s):** Sweeping down behind Aldric, descending to eye-level, and executing a tight 360-degree arc around his upper body as he continues moving forward.
  * **Phase C (6s–10s):** Settles into a steady tracking position directly behind Aldric—elevated 5 feet off the ground and angled slightly upward—framing Aldric in the foreground with the looming 8-foot blue stone walls of Node H (North Entrance) filling the background.
