# LIFESPAN AND AGING

## Overview

The peoples of this world possess significantly longer lifespans than those of ordinary Earth humans.

This long lifespan is believed to be a natural consequence of the world's magical environment.

Both Awakened and Unawakened individuals generally live much longer than expected.

---

# AVERAGE LIFESPAN

Average Human Lifespan:

200 Years

This applies to most healthy individuals regardless of social class.

---

# AGING SCALE

### Young Child

0 - 15 Years

---

### Youth

16 - 40 Years

Newly Awakened individuals typically belong to this age range.

---

### Young Adult

40 - 75 Years

Considered fully mature adults.

Most people begin careers and families during this period.

---

### Middle Age

75 - 125 Years

Peak experience and influence.

Many leaders emerge during this age.

---

### Senior

125 - 175 Years

Respected elders.

Often hold leadership positions.

---

### Elder

175 - 220 Years

Advanced age.

Physical decline becomes noticeable.

---

### Exceptional Longevity

220+ Years

Rare but achievable.

Often associated with:

- Powerful Awakened individuals
- Ancient bloodlines
- Magical enhancement
- Legendary achievements

# POLITICAL EFFECTS

Long lifespans influence society.

Examples:

- Kings may reign for over a century.
- Blood feuds can last generations.
- Ancient grudges remain active.
- Historical events remain within living memory.

People routinely meet individuals who remember events occurring over 100 years ago.

---

# HISTORICAL MEMORY

Individuals over 150 years old may personally remember events considered ancient history by younger generations.

This creates conflicts between:

- Official history
- Living witnesses
- Political propaganda

The Ancient Warlock Empire remains old enough that no living person remembers its fall directly.

---

# CANON RULES

The AI must consider lifespan when creating:

- Relationships
- Politics
- Leadership
- Historical events
- Character behavior

A 100-year-old character should not automatically be treated as elderly.

A 50-year-old character should never be described as old.

Character maturity should reflect the world's lifespan rather than Earth standards.
