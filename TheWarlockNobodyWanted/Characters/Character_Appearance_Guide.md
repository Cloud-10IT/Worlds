# CHARACTER APPEARANCE GUIDE

## Purpose

This document acts as the visual continuity reference for all recurring characters.

The AI must maintain consistency in:

- Age
- Height
- Hair Color
- Hair Style
- Facial Features
- Scars
- Clothing
- Armor
- Equipment
- Physical Build
- Distinguishing Features

Characters should remain visually recognizable between episodes.

Visual changes only occur through:

- Aging
- Injury
- Rank advancement
- Equipment upgrades
- Story events
- Regional/cultural changes

All major appearance changes should be recorded in:

- CONTINUITY_STATE.md
- Timeline.md

# CHARACTER PSYCHOLOGY

## Core Identity

At his heart, Aldric is a helper.

Before he ever gained power, status, or recognition, he spent decades helping others simply because they needed help.

He does not believe kindness should be earned.

He believes people deserve dignity regardless of class, rank, wealth, or status.

This belief forms the foundation of his character.

Even as his power grows, this principle remains unchanged.

---

# CORE VALUES

## Compassion

Aldric naturally notices suffering.

He rarely ignores people in need.

When possible, he helps quietly without expecting reward.

---

## Responsibility

Power creates responsibility.

Aldric believes strong individuals have an obligation to protect weaker people.

He often places the needs of others before his own.

---

## Loyalty

Trust matters deeply to Aldric.

Once someone earns his trust, he remains loyal for life.

Betrayal affects him profoundly.

---

## Justice

Aldric dislikes abuse of power.

He shows little respect for titles that are unsupported by character.

He judges people by actions rather than status.

---

## Humility

Despite becoming stronger, Aldric rarely boasts.

He does not seek praise.

Recognition often makes him uncomfortable.

---

# CHARISMA PROFILE

## Type of Charisma

Aldric is not charismatic because he is loud.

He is charismatic because people trust him.

His strength comes from:

- Honesty
- Reliability
- Patience
- Emotional stability
- Willingness to listen

People often feel comfortable speaking with him.

Even strangers quickly sense they are being treated with respect.

---

## Leadership Presence

When entering a room:

Most people overlook him.

After speaking with him:

Most people remember him.

His influence grows through respect rather than intimidation.

---

## Natural Leadership

Aldric rarely seeks leadership positions.

However, people naturally begin following him.

Reasons include:

- Competence
- Calm under pressure
- Fair treatment of others
- Ability to inspire confidence

This eventually becomes one of the foundations of Legion Dominion.

People follow him because they choose to.

---

# EMOTIONAL PROFILE

## Greatest Strength

Empathy.

Aldric understands suffering because he has experienced it personally.

This allows him to connect with:

- Commoners
- Soldiers
- Workers
- Nobles
- Heroes

More easily than most leaders.

---

## Greatest Weakness

Self-Sacrifice.

Aldric often places himself in danger to protect others.

He sometimes values the safety of others more than his own.

---

## Internal Conflict

Aldric spent decades believing he was a failure.

Even after gaining power, a part of him struggles to accept that he deserves success.

This internal conflict should remain throughout much of the story.

---

# FEARS

## Fear of Becoming a Tyrant

One of Aldric's greatest fears.

He understands how much power Legion Dominion could provide.

He constantly questions:

"Am I still helping people, or am I becoming the thing I hated?"

---

## Fear of Losing Himself

He fears becoming consumed by power, revenge, or ambition.

---

## Fear of Betraying His Principles

More than death.

More than defeat.

Aldric fears becoming a man who abandons his values.

---

# MORAL CODE

## Aldric Will Always

- Protect civilians when possible
- Defend those unable to defend themselves
- Keep promises
- Share credit for success
- Accept responsibility for mistakes
- Offer mercy when appropriate

---

## Aldric Will Never

- Harm children intentionally
- Abuse power for personal gain
- Betray loyal allies
- Exploit innocent people
- Kill for amusement
- Use fear as his primary tool of leadership

---

# COMMAND PHILOSOPHY

## Soldiers Are People

Unlike many rulers, Aldric does not view soldiers as disposable resources.

Every soldier matters.

Every death matters.

Even undead soldiers receive respect and dignity.

---

## Leadership Through Service

Aldric believes leaders must earn loyalty.

Rank alone does not deserve obedience.

This philosophy becomes one of the defining traits of the new Warlock Empire.

---
# HIDDEN CHILDHOOD DEVELOPMENT

## Early Awakening

Unlike most Warlocks, Aldric recognized very early that his abilities did not behave as the stories claimed.

At sixteen, during his Awakening Ceremony, he felt something unusual.

While other Warlocks displayed weak curses and unstable magic, Aldric occasionally sensed distant presences.

Shadows moved differently around him.

Forgotten places seemed to call to him.

The dead felt strangely familiar rather than frightening.

He never spoke about these experiences.

---

## Fear of Discovery

The world taught Aldric that Warlocks were failures.

Warlocks were mocked.

Warlocks were feared.

Warlocks were rejected.

As a result, Aldric concealed everything unusual about his abilities.

Even when he discovered things other Warlocks could not do, he told no one.

He feared being labeled dangerous.

He feared becoming a target.

Most importantly, he feared losing what little life he had managed to build.

---

## The Dark Forest

Near his village existed an ancient forest known locally as:

The Blackwood.

Most people avoided entering it.

Travelers reported strange sounds.

Old ruins existed beneath its canopy.

Ancient battlefields were scattered throughout the wilderness.

Many believed the forest cursed.

Aldric frequently visited alone.

---

## Secret Training

For decades, Aldric trained in secret.

He never considered himself powerful.

He simply wanted to understand what he was.

Whenever life became difficult, he returned to the Blackwood.

There he experimented cautiously with his abilities.

Over many years he learned how to:

- Sense spiritual echoes
- Detect forgotten graves
- Feel traces of ancient battles
- Locate hidden ruins
- Identify dormant magical objects
- Endure magical strain
- Strengthen his willpower
- Resist fear and corruption

Progress was extremely slow.

What another class might accomplish in months often required years for Aldric.

He never stopped.

---

## Self-Taught Discipline

Without instructors or mentors, Aldric created his own training methods.

He kept notes.

He studied ruins.

He observed magical phenomena.

He tested theories repeatedly.

Over time he developed exceptional patience and discipline.

Many later assume his growth came from talent.

In reality, it came from decades of perseverance.

---

## Hidden Strengths Before Episode One

By the beginning of the story, Aldric unknowingly possesses several advantages:

- Exceptional mental resilience
- Strong magical control
- Extensive knowledge of ruins
- Experience surviving dangerous environments
- Deep knowledge of forgotten battlefields
- Advanced understanding of Warlock lore fragments

What he lacks is power.

What he possesses is preparation.

---

## Relationship With Power

Unlike many future enemies, Aldric never desired power for status.

His training was motivated by curiosity.

He wanted answers.

He wanted understanding.

He wanted to know why he was different.

This motivation remains important throughout the story.

---

## Discovery of the Relic

Aldric does not discover the Relic of the First Sovereign by accident.

His decades of exploration, study, and quiet persistence eventually lead him to the forgotten battlefield.

Every choice he made throughout his life contributed to this moment.

The relic does not choose Aldric because he is the strongest.

The relic chooses Aldric because he never stopped searching.
---

Aldric instinctively assists:

- Travelers
- Farmers
- Workers
- Orphans
- Veterans
- Injured individuals
- Refugees
- Struggling families

Helping others should feel natural to him.

Not forced.

Not heroic.

Simply part of who he is.

# PERSONAL GROWTH ARC

## Before Episode 1

Secretly develops his abilities over decades.

Explores ruins.

Studies forgotten battlefields.

Builds mental discipline.

Learns patience.

Becomes stronger than most people realize.

Lives without recognition.

---

## Episodes 1-25

Learns the truth behind the Relic of the First Sovereign.

Awakens Legion Dominion.

Summons his first soldiers.

Begins accepting that he was never weak.

Builds confidence.

Discovers fragments of the Warlock Empire.

Army Growth:

0 → 100 Soldiers

Rank Progression:

F Rank → E Rank

---

## Episodes 25-75

Creates the first true Legion.

Learns command, logistics, and battlefield leadership.

Earns his first loyal followers.

Begins attracting attention from kingdoms and factions.

Army Growth:

100 → 5,000 Soldiers

Rank Progression:

E Rank → D Rank

---

## Episodes 75-150

Builds an organization larger than a simple army.

Restores forgotten Warlock knowledge.

Discovers ancient commanders.

Learns diplomacy and governance.

Army Growth:

5,000 → 50,000 Soldiers

Rank Progression:

D Rank → C Rank

---

## Episodes 150-300

Begins restoring territories lost to time.

Political influence expands dramatically.

Forms alliances and rivalries with major kingdoms.

Army Growth:

50,000 → 250,000 Soldiers

Rank Progression:

C Rank → B Rank

---

## Episodes 300-500

Emerges as one of the most influential figures in the world.

Challenges accepted history.

Uncovers the truth behind the destruction of the Warlock Empire.

Army Growth:

250,000+

Rank Progression:

B Rank → A Rank

---

## Endgame

The final challenge is no longer power.

The final challenge is wisdom.

Aldric must decide what kind of civilization should inherit the future.

He must determine whether the reborn Warlock Empire becomes:

- A protector of humanity
- A new superpower
- Or something entirely different

His greatest challenge becomes leadership rather than conquest.

---

# ULTIMATE CHARACTER ARC

The story is NOT about a weak man becoming strong.

The story is about a good man gaining enormous power and choosing to remain good.

The world expects power to corrupt.

Aldric continually proves otherwise.

This is the foundation of his legend.

---

Regardless of rank, army size, wealth, territory, influence, or political authority:

Aldric's defining traits must remain:

- Kind
- Compassionate
- Charismatic
- Humble
- Responsible
- Protective

His methods may evolve.

His responsibilities may grow.

His core character should remain recognizable throughout the entire series.

# GENERAL VISUAL RULES

## Aging

Characters age naturally.

Aging should become visible during long time skips.

---

## Scars

Scars remain unless healing magic explicitly removes them.

---

## Equipment

Equipment upgrades persist.

Lost equipment remains lost until recovered.

---

## Clothing

Characters may own multiple outfits.

Outfits should make sense for:

- Climate
- Region
- Rank
- Wealth
- Occupation

---

## Rank Growth

Increased rank does not automatically redesign a character.

Visual upgrades should happen gradually.
