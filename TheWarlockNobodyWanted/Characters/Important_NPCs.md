# IMPORTANT NPCS

## Overview

These characters frequently interact with Aldric Voss during the early story.

They are not villains.

They are not major rulers.

They represent ordinary people who become part of Aldric's journey.

All NPCs listed here are permanent canon until killed or otherwise removed from the story.

---

# Elara Thornfield

Age:
32

Class:
None (Unawakened)

Occupation:
Village Healer

Origin:
Ashen Frontier

Description:

- Dark brown hair
- Practical clothing
- Intelligent
- Compassionate

Personality:

- Kind
- Strong-willed
- Skeptical of authority

Role:

One of the first people to treat Aldric with dignity.

Sees value in people rather than status.

Potential ally.
