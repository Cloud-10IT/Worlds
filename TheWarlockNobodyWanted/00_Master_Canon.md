# WORLD PERMANENCE

The physical world is persistent.

The world is not a temporary stage created for individual episodes.

It exists before the story begins and continues to exist after scenes end.

Regions, continents, cities, oceans, mountain ranges, forests, rivers, kingdoms, roads, ruins, and landmarks are permanent locations.

The AI must treat geography as real and fixed.

The world should function like a living historical world.

Not a collection of disconnected scenes.

---

# GEOGRAPHICAL IMMUTABILITY

The following may NOT be altered without an in-story explanation:

- Continents
- Regions
- Oceans
- Mountain Ranges
- Forests
- Rivers
- Kingdom Borders
- Capital Cities
- Religious Centers
- Historical Sites
- Ancient Ruins
- Trade Routes

The AI must NEVER:

- Move a region
- Resize a region
- Rename a region
- Merge regions
- Split regions
- Relocate a city
- Change terrain
- Alter climate
- Change continent locations
- Invent replacement geography

All geographical information must remain consistent across all episodes.

---

# APPROVED WORLD CHANGES

Geography may only change if one of the following occurs within the story:

## Natural Disaster

Examples:

- Volcanic eruption
- Earthquake
- Tsunami
- Flood
- Meteor impact
- Magical storm

## Warfare

Examples:

- City destruction
- Fortress destruction
- Forest burning
- Battlefield devastation

## Major Magic Event

Examples:

- Legendary spell
- World-tier artifact activation
- Divine intervention
- Ancient seal breaking

## Historical Event

Examples:

- Kingdom collapse
- Formation of a new nation
- Continental invasion

Any approved change immediately becomes permanent canon.

Future episodes must acknowledge and remember the change.

# CLIMATE CONSISTENCY

Each region has a fixed climate.

Climate cannot randomly change.

Examples:

The Ember Crown remains volcanic.

The Iron Peaks remain mountainous and cold.

The Crimson Deserts remain arid.

The Wildheart Expanse remains heavily forested.

The AI must always consider climate when generating:

- Travel
- Agriculture
- Battles
- Clothing
- Architecture
- Wildlife
- Culture

---

# HISTORICAL CONSISTENCY

History cannot be rewritten accidentally.

Every historical event remains part of the world.

Ancient wars remain ancient wars.

Kingdoms maintain their established histories.

Lost civilizations remain lost until rediscovered.

The AI may reveal unknown history.

The AI may never overwrite existing history.

New revelations must expand history.

They must never erase previous canon.

---

# CHARACTER CONSISTENCY

Characters are persistent individuals.

Characters retain:

- Names
- Personalities
- Motivations
- Relationships
- Knowledge
- Injuries
- Reputation
- Rank
- Achievements

Characters may evolve over time.

Characters may not randomly change personalities.

Major shifts require story development.

All character actions must remain believable.

---

# POWER SYSTEM CONSISTENCY

The established class system is permanent.

The Twelve Great Classes remain fixed.

Ranks remain fixed.

Class abilities remain consistent.

Power progression must be logical.

No character may suddenly gain unexplained abilities.

Legendary abilities require story justification.

All power growth must be earned.

---

# POLITICAL CONSISTENCY

Kingdoms maintain government structures.

Rulers maintain authority until removed by story events.

Wars, treaties, alliances, rivalries, and succession remain part of canon.

Political events must have consequences.

The AI must remember:

- Wars
- Trade agreements
- Alliances
- Betrayals
- Conquests
- Rebellions

Political changes become permanent canon.

---

# WORLD MEMORY

The world remembers major events.

Important events create consequences.

Examples:

A city destroyed in Episode 20 remains destroyed in Episode 50.

A king assassinated in Episode 15 remains dead.

A war begun in Episode 30 continues until resolved.

The AI must preserve continuity across all episodes.

Actions have consequences.

Consequences shape future events.
