# ARTIFACTS

## Overview

Artifacts are magical objects that contain ancient power.

Most artifacts originate from lost civilizations.

Artifacts range from simple enchanted objects to world-changing relics.

---

# ARTIFACT TIERS

## Common
Minor magical effects. Usually produced by modern craftsmen.

## Rare
Powerful enchantments. Valuable to adventurers and nobles.

## Epic
Ancient treasures. Capable of dramatically enhancing abilities.

## Legendary
Extremely rare. Often tied to historical events. Kingdom-level importance.

## Mythic
Believed lost. Capable of influencing the fate of entire regions.

## World Relics
Unique objects. Only one may exist. Capable of permanently altering history.
