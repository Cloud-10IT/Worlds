# POWER SYSTEM

## Overview

All Awakened individuals possess a Class.

A Class grants access to abilities unavailable to ordinary people.

Power is divided into Ranks.

Rank determines the strength, control, efficiency, and scale of a person's abilities.

A higher rank does not automatically guarantee victory.

Experience, strategy, equipment, terrain, and preparation remain important.

---

# THE RANK SYSTEM

## F Rank

Newly awakened.

Limited understanding of abilities.

Minimal combat capability.

---

## E Rank

Basic mastery.

Able to support adventuring parties, military units, and local organizations.

---

## D Rank

Professional level.

Most successful adventurers and soldiers remain at this rank for life.

Represents competence and battlefield reliability.

---

## C Rank

Veteran level.

Known beyond local regions.

Often hold positions of authority within guilds and armies.

---

## B Rank

Elite level.

Capable of influencing major battles.

Frequently command military units and elite adventuring groups.

---

## A Rank

National Asset.

Kingdoms actively seek their loyalty.

Can alter the outcome of wars.

---

## S Rank

Legendary individuals.

Few exist within an entire generation.

Capable of changing the fate of nations.

---

## Legend Rank

Historical figures.

Remembered for centuries.

Often associated with world-changing events.

---

## Mythic Rank

Individuals whose existence becomes legend.

Capable of feats beyond conventional understanding.

---

## Transcendent Rank

Believed to be impossible.

No confirmed records exist within modern history.

---

# PROGRESSION

Ranks cannot be purchased.

Ranks cannot be inherited.

True advancement requires:

- Training
- Combat experience
- Discovery
- Knowledge
- Growth
- Sacrifice

All progression must be earned.

Sudden unexplained power growth is forbidden.

---

# CLASS RESTRICTIONS

Classes possess different strengths and weaknesses.

No class is perfect.

No class is automatically superior in every situation.

Abilities must remain consistent with established canon.
