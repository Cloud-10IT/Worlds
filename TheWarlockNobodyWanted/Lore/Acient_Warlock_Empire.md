# ANCIENT WARLOCK EMPIRE

## Canon Status

The Ancient Warlock Empire is one of the foundational mysteries of the world.

Most official records deny its existence.

Many historians believe stories about the empire are myths.

The truth has been intentionally hidden for over one thousand years.

This document contains both known facts and hidden truths.

The AI must treat all information within this file as canon.

---

# OVERVIEW

The Ancient Warlock Empire was the largest civilization ever recorded in human history.

At its height, the empire spanned multiple continents, controlled vast military forces, and maintained relative peace for centuries.

Unlike modern kingdoms, the empire was not built solely through conquest.

It was built through unity.

The Warlock Sovereigns believed that strength existed to protect civilization rather than dominate it.

This philosophy eventually became known as:

The Sovereign Doctrine.

---

# OFFICIAL HISTORY

Modern history teaches:

- Warlocks were weak.
- Warlocks were dangerous.
- Warlocks were failures.
- Warlocks never ruled nations.
- The Ancient Warlock Empire never existed.

These teachings are false.

They were deliberately created after the Empire's collapse.

---

# THE HIDDEN TRUTH

The original Warlocks were not demon summoners.

The original Warlocks were Sovereigns.

A Sovereign's power was not measured by personal combat ability.

Their strength came from:

- Leadership
- Loyalty
- Organization
- Command
- Unity

Their ultimate authority was known as:

LEGION DOMINION

Through this power they could:

- Command armies
- Inspire loyalty
- Raise battlefield legions
- Preserve military knowledge
- Unite people from different classes and regions

The Warlocks became the architects of civilization.

---

# FOUNDING OF THE EMPIRE

Approximate Time:

Class:

Warlock

Legend Rank

Title:

First Sovereign

# AETHEL SOVEREIGN

Aethel was the first individual to fully master Legion Dominion.

Unlike other rulers, Aethel did not seek conquest for personal glory.

He sought stability.

Many regions were consumed by endless warfare.

Cities rose and fell constantly.

Civilization remained fragile.

Aethel united scattered kingdoms beneath a single banner.

Over time he became known as:

The First Sovereign.

---

# THE SOVEREIGN DOCTRINE

Core Principles:

1. Power Protects.
2. Leadership Is Service.
3. Strength Creates Responsibility.
4. No Life Is Disposable.
5. Unity Is Greater Than Division.

These values became the foundation of Imperial society.

---

# IMPERIAL GOVERNMENT

The Empire was divided into Provinces.

Each Province possessed significant autonomy.

Rather than ruling through fear, the Sovereigns governed through oaths, alliances, and service.

Regional leaders represented many classes.

Examples:

- Warriors
- Paladins
- Druids
- Hunters
- Priests
- Mages

The Empire welcomed all classes.

---

# THE GOLDEN AGE

For centuries the Empire flourished.

Major achievements included:

- Continental road networks
- Grand libraries
- Military academies
- Unified trade systems
- Magical research
- Agricultural expansion

Many modern technologies and magical traditions originated during this period.

Most nations simply no longer remember their origins.

---

# THE LEGIONS

The Empire maintained several legendary military forces.

Each Legion possessed unique responsibilities.

Examples:

- Frontier Defense
- Exploration
- Siege Warfare
- Intelligence Operations
- Monster Suppression

Many forgotten military ruins throughout the world once belonged to these Legions.

Contrary to modern propaganda, the Empire did not suppress other classes.

- Warriors
- Hunters

# THE FIRST CRACKS

As the Empire expanded, fear began to grow.

Some individuals viewed the Sovereigns as too powerful.

Others feared Legion Dominion itself.

Questions emerged:

"What happens if a Sovereign becomes corrupt?"

"What happens if this power falls into the wrong hands?"

These fears slowly spread.

---

# THE GREAT BETRAYAL

Historical records are fragmented.

Details remain unclear.

Known facts:

- A coalition formed against the Empire.
- Multiple powerful factions participated.
- Several high-ranking leaders betrayed the Sovereigns.
- A massive war followed.

The war became known as:

The Sundering War.

---

# THE SUNDERING WAR

The largest conflict in recorded history.

Entire cities vanished.

Kingdoms collapsed.

Ancient weapons were unleashed.

Millions died.

Many historical records were destroyed during the conflict.

---

# FALL OF THE EMPIRE

The final Sovereigns disappeared.

Many were killed.

Others vanished.

Their fate remains unknown.

The Empire fragmented.

Its cities fell into ruin.

Its roads vanished beneath forests and deserts.

Its history was systematically erased.

---

# THE ERASURE

Following the Empire's collapse:

- Libraries were destroyed.
- Historical records vanished.
- Relics were hidden.
- Knowledge was suppressed.

Over generations the truth faded.

The mighty Sovereigns became remembered as weak Warlocks.

One of the most successful propaganda campaigns in history had begun.

---
