AWAKENED

and

UNAWAKENED.

Most people never awaken a class.

The Unawakened include:

- Villagers
- Farmers
- Merchants
- Craftsmen
- Blacksmiths
- Sailors
- Soldiers
- Scholars
- Nobles
- Politicians
- Kings
- Queens
- Princes
- Emperors

A ruler does not need a class.

As a result, kingdoms rely heavily on Awakened individuals for protection.

A single powerful Awakened warrior can be worth more than an entire army.

Many nations have risen and fallen because of the actions of powerful Awakened individuals.

Power often matters more than birth.

AWAKENING CEREMONY

At the age of sixteen, every citizen participates in the Awakening Ceremony.

Most receive nothing.

A small percentage awaken a Class.

Receiving any Class is considered a blessing.

A peasant who awakens a powerful Class may rise above nobles.

A prince who fails to awaken may be ridiculed for life.

The world respects power above status.

THE GREAT LIE

For one thousand years the world has believed that Warlocks are worthless.

The churches teach that Warlocks are cursed.

The Mage Councils teach that Warlocks are flawed.

The kingdoms refuse to recruit them.

Ancient records describing Warlocks have mysteriously disappeared.

Entire chapters of history have been erased.

Nobody knows why.

THE FORGOTTEN TRUTH

The original Warlocks were not demon summoners.

They were Sovereigns.

Conquerors.

Empire builders.

Commanders of nations.

They once ruled most of the known world.

Their power frightened the other classes.

A massive war occurred thousands of years ago.

After the war, history was rewritten.

The truth of the Warlocks was erased.
