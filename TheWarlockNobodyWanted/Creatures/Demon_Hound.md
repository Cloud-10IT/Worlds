# DEMON HOUND

## Identity

Name: Demon Hound

Classification: Bound Summoned Beast

Species: Demonic Canine

Type: Arcane Summon

Status: Active Summon

Primary Summoner: Aldric Voss

---

## Core Appearance

The Demon Hound is a gigantic supernatural canine created through ancient summoning magic.

The creature possesses:

- Massive muscular body
- Thick powerful limbs
- Large clawed paws
- Broad chest
- Long tail
- Predatory stance
- Intimidating silhouette

The Demon Hound should appear capable of tearing through entire groups of enemies with brute force.

The Demon Hound is a summoned creature and must constantly display evidence of its magical nature.

Required Effects:

- Permanent white mist
- Constant spectral fog
- Ethereal vapor trails
- Arcane particles
- Subtle magical glow

The mist should always surround the creature.

The mist should drift naturally around:

- Legs
- Shoulders
- Back
- Tail

Even when standing still.

The mist is a permanent visual effect.

A gigantic Gray White Demon Hound stands beside Aldric Voss. Thick fur ripples in the wind while two massive black horns curve backward along its skull toward its back.
