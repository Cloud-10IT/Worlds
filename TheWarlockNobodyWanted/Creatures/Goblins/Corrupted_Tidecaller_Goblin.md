# Overview

Corrupted Tidecallers are among the rarest and most feared goblin variants in existence.

Most scholars believe they originate from Deepwater Shamans who become exposed to ancient abyssal forces beneath the Shadow Coast.

Over decades or centuries, their bodies, minds, and souls become altered by entities from the Deep Deep.

The resulting creature is no longer truly goblin.

It becomes something else.
