# Overview

Sea Kings are rulers of the largest goblin kingdoms found along the Shadow Coast.

Unlike common goblins, Sea Kings think in terms of kingdoms, generations, and conquest.

A single Sea King may reign for centuries.

Entire towns and ports may disappear under their rule.
