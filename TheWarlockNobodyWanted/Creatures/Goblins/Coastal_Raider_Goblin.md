Goblin

Low

Raider

Population Frequency:
Very Common

# Appearance

Height:
3.5 to 4.5 feet

Build:
Lean and wiry

Skin:
Blue-gray

Eyes:
Yellow

Hair:
Black or dark brown

Features:

- Salt-weathered skin
- Scarred appearance
- Webbed fingers
- Large ears
- Sharp fangs

---

# Equipment

Common Weapons:

- Rusted cutlass
- Harpoon spear
- Fishing knife

Common Armor:

- Leather armor
- Salt-stained cloth
- Fish-hide padding

Common Shields:

- Driftwood shield
- Repaired wooden shield

# Combat Style

Fights in groups.

Relies on intimidation and numbers.

Prefers attacking:

- Fishermen
- Traders
- Villages

Avoids prolonged combat.

---

# Strengths

- Swimming
- Cliff climbing
- Coastal navigation
- Fighting in fog

---

# Weaknesses

- Low discipline
- Poor equipment
- Weak morale

---

# Intelligence

Primitive

Can:

- Follow orders
- Use basic tactics
- Organize raids

Cannot:

- Conduct campaigns
- Lead armies
