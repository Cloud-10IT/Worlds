# GOBLINS

## Species Overview

Goblins are the most common hostile humanoid species found throughout Aeloria. While individually weak, goblins become extremely dangerous when organized into tribes, warbands, kingdoms, or empires under powerful leaders.

Goblin intelligence varies greatly by rank. Lower goblins act primarily on instinct and pack behavior, while higher-ranking goblins can rival human generals, kings, and master strategists.

Goblins reproduce rapidly, mature quickly, and adapt to nearly any environment. Throughout history they have been responsible for countless raids, wars, and regional collapses.
