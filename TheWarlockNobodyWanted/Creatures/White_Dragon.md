# WHITE DRAGON

## Identity

Name: White Dragon

Classification: Ancient Arcane Dragon

Species: Dragon

Alignment: Bound Summoned Creature

Status: Active Summon

Primary Summoner: Aldric Voss

---

## Core Appearance

The White Dragon is a gigantic ancient dragon with a powerful and intimidating presence.

The dragon possesses:

- Snow white scales
- Massive muscular body
- Long powerful neck
- Broad chest
- Thick armored limbs
- Large clawed feet
- Long scaled tail
- Gigantic wingspan

The dragon should appear capable of destroying walls and towers through physical power alone.

The creature must appear ancient, powerful, and intelligent.

---

## Head Design

The dragon's head is distinctive and must remain consistent.

Features:

- Elongated dragon skull
- Noble facial structure
- Large black horns
- Horns curve backward
- Smaller secondary horn ridges
- Strong jawline
- Exposed sharp fangs
- Defined cheek ridges

The horn shape must never change.

The dragon must always possess black horns.

---

## Eyes

Eye Color:

Violet

Appearance:

- Bright glowing violet irises
- Magical inner glow
- Intelligent expression
- Ancient wisdom

The eye color is mandatory.

Never replace violet eyes.

---

## Scale Appearance

Scale Color:

Pure snow white

Surface:

- Highly detailed scales
- Subtle reflective properties
- Realistic surface texture
- Thick armored plating

Scale Rules:

- Never become silver
- Never become blue
- Never become gray
- Never become gold

The dragon must remain white.

---

## Wing Design

The wings are massive.

Requirements:

- Membranous wings
- White scaled support structure
- Powerful musculature
- Large enough for long distance flight

When extended:

- Cast large shadows
- Create visible air displacement
- Affect nearby foliage and cloth

---

Effects:

- Violet energy particles
- Arcane mist
- Soft magical glow
- Floating energy fragments
- Subtle rune manifestations

Magic Color:

Violet

Magic color must remain consistent with Aldric Voss.
