# LOCATION: BRADENWOOD TOWN
**Region:** Shadow Coast  
**Terrain:** Coastal Shoreline, Forested Borders, Cobblestone Terrain  
**Defensive Status:** High Security (Fortified Perimeter)  

---

## OVERVIEW
Located along the rugged shoreline of the Shadow Coast, Bradenwood is a heavily fortified, stone-paved coastal town engineered to withstand both maritime storms and hostile invasions.

---

## ARCHITECTURE & DEFENSIVE PERIMETER

* **Perimeter Wall:** A solid, 8-foot-tall defensive wall constructed entirely from deep-hued blue stone surrounds the town perimeter.
* **Rampart Parapet:** The blue stone wall is engineered wide enough for 3 men to walk side by side along the battlements for patrols and defensive maneuvering.
* **Street Paving:** Tightly laid, uniform cobblestone covers every street and walkway throughout the interior.
* **Town Entrances:** Three primary gates grant entry into the town, positioned at the **North**, **East**, and **South** perimeter walls.
* **Guard Deployment:** Each gate maintains a dedicated guardhouse staffed by a 3-guard detachment:
  * 2 guards posted at ground level monitoring gate traffic.
  * 1 guard stationed directly above on the blue stone wall on lookout duty.

---

## DISTRICTS & KEY LOCATIONS

### 1. The Waterfront (West Side)
* **The Port:** Dominates the entire western edge of Bradenwood, handling incoming cargo, fishing fleets, and regional sea trade along the Shadow Coast.
* **The Lighthouse:** Positioned just past the port on its southern flank, housing a high-elevation beacon to guide ships through coastal fog and sea shoals.
* **The Dockside Tavern:** Situated directly adjacent to the port, catering to sailors, dockworkers, and sea merchants.

### 2. Commercial & Artisan Quarter
* **The Blacksmith:** Central forge supplying tools, ship fittings, and weapons for the town guard.
* **The Clothier:** Main-street tailor crafting garments for townspeople and traveling merchant captains.
* **The Jeweler:** High-end artisan dealing in refined metals and precious gems.

### 3. Industry & Residential Zones
* **Loggers Guild / Timber Yard:** Manages raw timber brought in from the surrounding forest borders.
* **Town Center Tavern:** Located deep in the cobblestone interior, serving local residents, loggers, and off-duty guards.
* **Residential Dwellings:** Standard stone and timber homes nestled tightly along the cobblestone grid between the commercial shops and the outer blue stone wall.
